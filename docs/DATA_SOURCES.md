## Redistribution boundaries

The original source licenses continue to apply to source data and code. No blanket license is applied to third-party material. The Indian replication archive is CC0; the two Mendeley datasets are CC BY 4.0, with attribution above. Official policy documents and company annual reports retain their source terms.

Licensed LSEG microdata, firm-level LSEG panels, private identity maps, complete company-report PDFs and full-page report excerpts are excluded. The retained LSEG results are aggregate tables and estimates. Public-source firm identifiers belong to the separately constructed Chinese analysis, not to an LSEG extract.

The original article DOCX/PDF and authoring drafts are not part of this code-and-data repository. The repository title matches the manuscript's final title.
