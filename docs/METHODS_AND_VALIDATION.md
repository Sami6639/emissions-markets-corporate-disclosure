## Protocol and sensitivity

The China main protocol and conservative-control/baseline-industry amendment preceded the new coefficients. They are internal records, not external preregistration. Overlap weighting was added after the balance audit and is marked accordingly. All original comparator, unbalanced, dictionary, fixed-effect, covariate, cluster and province-omission results are retained. The constant-linear-drift calculation is not claimed as a full HonestDiD implementation.
The final referee audit adds full-design CR2/Satterthwaite inference (df 5.479; 95% CI [0.267855,0.948135]), a cluster jackknife and treatment-weight diagnostics. Planned sector checks retain a positive chemical-only result (0.591281; 95% CI [0.207875,0.974687]); coal-only, paper-only and exclusion of chemicals have insufficient support for the maintained inference. Three treated post cells have negative regression weights, total mass -0.008282; the fixed-effect coefficient is not an unweighted ATT under heterogeneity.
Exact-date policy assignment uses initial roster inclusion, not later transaction activity. The 2017 framework and January 2020 accounting rule precede allocation and operational trading.
